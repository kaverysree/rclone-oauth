Privacy Policy

SyncFilesBruh is a personal Google Drive synchronization utility operated for the owner's own private research backup and file synchronization.

Google Drive data is accessed only when necessary to list, read, upload, update, or synchronize files requested by the owner.

SyncFilesBruh does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.

OAuth credentials and synchronization state are stored only on systems controlled by the owner. Data transferred by the application flows only between those systems and the owner's Google Drive account.

The owner can revoke SyncFilesBruh's Google account access at any time from the Google Account security settings and can delete locally stored credentials and synchronized files.

This application is for personal use and is not offered as a public service.
